# Economic Cycle Research — Version 6.3 Roadmap

**Status:** PLANNED — not authorized to displace V6.2 production stability.
**Baseline:** V6.2 production application, source-qualified economic judgment and eleven sector pages.
**Theme:** Better economic judgment, more useful sector intelligence, and measured accuracy — without a new pipeline.

## Objective and product outcome
An everyday reader should understand: (1) what the latest qualified evidence says about the economy; (2) our directional, conditional interpretation and what would change it; (3) which sectors warrant closer research and why; and (4) what evidence is missing before any investment-fit conclusion. Detailed source dates, conflicting observations, historical comparisons, and methodology remain on research pages.

**Operating rule:** DAILY DATA. MONTHLY CONCLUSIONS. ONE DASHBOARD.
Retain the existing pipeline, GitHub Pages deployment, publication manifest, V6.2 preservation/rollback safeguards, and eleven-sector coverage. No new autonomous forecasting system, unsupported crash odds, personalized portfolio instructions, or parallel research engine.

### M1 — Evidence and editorial contract (first development chunk; target 1 week)
- Inventory existing observations, dates, revisions, monthly assessments, sector artifacts, and data-quality gates; reuse current schemas.
- Define a small, testable interpretation rubric for expansion, deterioration, and mixed signals, including counterevidence, source freshness, confidence **in evidence coverage** (not event probabilities), and explicit reversal conditions.
- Make the dashboard explain which observations drive the view and what changed since the prior accepted monthly assessment.
- Acceptance: deterministic current/stale/missing/revised/contradictory fixtures; no false certainty, lookahead, or invented trend.

### M2 — Historical validation and interpretation quality (second chunk; target 1–2 weeks)
- Replay the rubric against dated historical snapshots **only where source vintage and availability are defensible**. Label retrospective non-point-in-time comparisons clearly.
- Evaluate interpretation consistency, false alarms, missed deterioration, and directional agreement with subsequent observed growth/labor/inflation developments.
- Separate recession confirmation delays from early evidence; exclude one-off 2020 pandemic mechanisms from generalized cycle conclusions or label separately.
- Produce a compact scorecard with coverage, sample size, limitations, and examples of changed or contradicted opinions.
- Acceptance: reproducible tests and a readable scorecard; no unvalidated prediction probabilities or fabricated accuracy.

### M3 — Eleven-sector research intelligence (third chunk; target 1–2 weeks)
- Link each sector to its historically observed economic sensitivities, current qualified macro signals, and contrary cases.
- Distinguish **economic exposure** from **investment attractiveness**. Add valuation, earnings, and relative-performance evidence only when source-qualified and date-aligned; otherwise show Insufficient Evidence.
- Explain sectors worth investigating, why, key risks, and what evidence would reverse that assessment. No personalized buy/sell/hold or allocation advice.
- Acceptance: all eleven sector pages, transparent source links, complete disclosure coverage, and fail-closed tests.

### M4 — Reader-first presentation and research drill-down (fourth chunk; target 1 week)
- Refine the existing homepage rather than redesigning: Where We Are; Where We're Heading; Where Investors Should Look.
- Add simple trend descriptions, dated monthly change summary, opposing evidence, and clear links to methodology and historical research.
- Preserve mobile-first navigation, collapsible research library, and one public dashboard.
- Acceptance: automated responsive/link checks at 360/768/desktop and recorded human accessibility review.

### M5 — Monthly operational proving and release (after next qualified monthly assessment; target 1 monthly cycle)
- Compare new conclusions with the previous accepted month, explain material changes, and retain the last accepted publication on stale/bad updates.
- Run complete unit/regression, V6.2 contract, sector, provenance, compliance, preservation, browser, and Pages publication checks.
- Verify the live release manifest, assessment date, exact originating commit, all eleven sectors, and rollback.
- Acceptance: one new qualified monthly assessment published, all gates documented, release notes and limitations complete, no release-blocking defects.

## V6.3 definition of done
1. All existing V6.2 source, freshness, preservation, disclosure, and release protections remain passing.
2. Dashboard presents clear source-qualified current judgment, conditional outlook, contrary evidence, reversal triggers, and dated monthly changes.
3. Historical scorecard discloses sample sizes, vintage limitations, false alarms and missed changes without unsupported predictive claims.
4. All eleven sectors have understandable economic context and properly gated investment-fit evidence.
5. Responsive, keyboard, contrast, and assistive-technology checks are documented.
6. Tests, monthly publication, live Pages, exact manifest/commit and rollback are verified.
7. README, release evidence, limitations and next backlog updated. Any failed gate means IN PROGRESS.

## Explicit exclusions
No new parallel pipelines, speculative market-crash timing, unsupported recession probabilities, individual-stock picks, personalized advice, monetization, autonomous AI agents, or major architecture rewrites.

## Engineering rules
Follow Karpathy-style discipline: inspect first; simplest sufficient implementation; reuse existing code; work in milestone-sized chunks; write tests with changes; verify actual behavior; minimize dependencies and user approvals. Preserve V6.2 as rollback baseline.
