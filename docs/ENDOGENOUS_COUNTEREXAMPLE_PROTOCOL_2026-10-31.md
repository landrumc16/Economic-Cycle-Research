# Historical counterexample design — endogenous crash threshold

Status: research protocol; no point-of-no-return claim.

## Candidate comparisons
- **1994–1996 tightening and soft landing:** documented recession non-event, NOT yet a certified equity drawdown non-event. Source: https://www.stlouisfed.org/publications/review/2025/apr/risk-management-monetary-policy-1994-95-fomc-tightening-episode
- **1983–1984 tightening:** documented soft-landing candidate; equity outcome pending independent price reconstruction. Source: https://www.stlouisfed.org/on-the-economy/2022/apr/fed-tightening-episodes-since-1980s-part-one
- **1998 Russia/LTCM:** stress-recovery candidate for U.S. economy, NOT a clean no-crash equity comparison; selected Nasdaq drawdown crossing appears in August 1998. Source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/powell20190823a.htm
- **2019 pre-pandemic slowdown:** right-censored by 2020 exogenous pandemic, not a verified no-crash observation. Source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/jefferson20240222a.htm

## Matching and falsification rules
1. Define index, adjusted price series, prior high, first 20% crossing and follow-up horizon consistently.
2. Match on pre-specified valuation, leverage, tightening, breadth and funding stress using only contemporaneously available data.
3. Censor follow-up at independently documented external-shock onset; never label shock-censored windows as normal-cycle successes.
4. Separate recession from equity drawdown outcomes.
5. Split parent events across training/holdout and source a complete event registry rather than eight selected cases.
6. Compare persistence and resolution of vulnerability, including high-fragility states that unwind without a 20% crossing.
7. Report denominator, selection effects, data gaps and uncertainty. No deterministic inevitability assertion.

## Immediate data gaps
- Independently verified index-specific daily adjusted price history and complete outcome registry.
- Publisher-original macro vintages and release timestamps.
- Comparable historical valuation, leverage and market breadth data.
- Independently adjudicated initiating mechanisms and external-shock censor dates.
