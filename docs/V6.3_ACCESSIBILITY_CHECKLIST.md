# V6.3 Accessibility Checklist

**Scope:** the 16 public V6.3 pages: overview, historical, sectors, monthly-research, research and sector-01 to sector-11.
**Status:** all automated checks PASS (including axe-core WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 A and AA, 0 violations). **Human checks NOT PERFORMED**: rows 1–4, 6 and 7 need a person or physical device; rows 5 and 8 have automated passes awaiting human confirmation.
