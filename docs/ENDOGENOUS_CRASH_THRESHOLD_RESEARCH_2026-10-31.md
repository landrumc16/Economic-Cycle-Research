# Endogenous market-crash threshold research — hypothesis and evaluation plan

Date: 2026-10-31
Status: RESEARCH DESIGN, NOT VALIDATED THRESHOLD
Research question: Is there a measurable pre-drawdown state of endogenous financial fragility beyond which a major decline is difficult to avert, or do seemingly similar conditions often unwind without a crash?

## Do not assume inevitability
Crashes recur, but no deterministic point of no return is established. Test a threshold hypothesis against gradual deflations, sideways consolidation, earnings catch-up, and false-positive episodes. Never label a threshold inevitable on historical retrospective correlation alone.

## Outcome definitions
Keep separate:
- Equity market drawdown: first crossing of -20% from a defined prior high, with index, adjusted prices, dates, recovery and observation horizon documented.
- Recession: independently dated business-cycle onset, distinct from market drawdowns.
- Financial-system crisis: independently sourced banking/credit distress criteria.
- Non-event: a prospectively specified horizon with complete observed outcomes; missing selected events does not imply no event.

## Mechanism taxonomy and exclusions
- Core endogenous cohort: speculative valuation/positioning, credit and leverage, liquidity/market structure, and monetary tightening interacting with domestic vulnerabilities.
- External shock cohort: pandemic 2020; exogenous war/natural disaster and comparable abrupt disruptions when the initiating shock is documented.
- Mixed/uncertain: ambiguous causes, multiple initiating mechanisms, and episodes whose origin cannot be independently classified.
- External shocks are excluded from *primary endogenous threshold estimation*, but retained for sensitivity analyses and fragility-amplification studies.
- Do not exclude a crash solely because policy, financial institutions, or human decisions contributed; those are often endogenous mechanisms.
- Record contemporaneous classification evidence, adjudication rules, conflicting interpretations and sensitivity to alternate labels.

## Candidate vulnerability dimensions
1. Equity valuation relative to earnings and discount rates.
2. Margin debt, corporate/household leverage and maturity mismatch.
3. Credit spreads, lending standards, defaults and funding stress.
4. Yield curve, real policy stance, monetary tightening and liquidity conditions.
5. Earnings breadth, labor deterioration and growth momentum.
6. Market breadth, concentration, speculative issuance and volatility/liquidity.

## Threshold falsification design
- Specify thresholds and horizons before scoring held-out eras; no tuning on the evaluation sample.
- Separate precursor state from *trigger*, and test interaction terms against simple one-factor baselines.
- Evaluate threshold persistence and conditional reversal; count cases of high fragility followed by recovery, not only crashes.
- Test distinct mechanisms and eras; avoid pooling external shocks into the endogenous target.
- Reconstruct publisher-original vintages and actual publication lags; prevent revised data and hindsight labels from entering signals.
- Use event-parent separation, chronological holdouts, base rates, lead-time distribution, precision/recall, false alerts per year, and calibration uncertainty.
- Explicitly test whether evidence supports only increasing risk, rather than inevitability.

## Current repository limitations
The existing 1987–2023 monthly workbook and eight selected market pathways are retrospective and not point-in-time certified. Selected drawdown crossings are not an exhaustive event registry; the apparent absence of a listed event cannot validate a negative outcome. Historical series before 1987 need sourcing. No crash probability or point-of-no-return assertion is authorized.

## First deliverable
A dated, source-audited event classification table (endogenous / external / mixed / uncertain), with event definition, initiating mechanism evidence, 20% crossing date, recession overlap, candidate precursor data availability, and reviewer notes. Then build matched no-crash comparison windows. Do not build another generic dashboard first.
