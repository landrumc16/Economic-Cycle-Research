# Economic Cycle Research — Delivery Roadmap to the Approved Final Product

**Status:** Proposed implementation sequence derived from docs/FINAL_PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md. Milestone scope requires owner approval before implementation.

## Strategy

Protect the working V6.1 baseline. Ship meaningful, independently testable releases. Reuse the canonical research pipeline and publication gate. Prefer a narrow reliable implementation over a sprawling incomplete one. Do not label a feature validated because its code executes.

## Phase 0 — Close V6.1 cleanly

- Confirm that deployed Pages actually contains the latest accepted V6.1 bundle, not a preserved older version.
- Archive tested commit, successful regression and publication run links, known limitations and rollback procedure.
- Maintain V6.1 as stable baseline; critical corrections only.

**Exit:** exact live artifact/version and public routes verified, closeout evidence archived.

## Phase 1 — V6.2: strengthen everyday usefulness

- Refine the three-question mobile homepage with explicit evidence freshness, dated official assessment, change-since-last-month and plain-language uncertainty.
- Improve navigation and collapsible research library without duplicating the data engine.
- Add visible educational-not-advice notices to investment and scenario surfaces.
- Provide a reproducible release manifest and deterministic smoke checks that confirm the *new* bundle, not merely successful preservation.

**Exit:** accessible mobile experience, verified live version, research traceability, automated smoke tests, disclaimer coverage.

## Phase 2 — Reliable historical and economic interpretation

- Reconcile independent historical episodes and non-downturn controls with source availability dates.
- Compare competing explanations and contradictory evidence; keep revised retrospective data separate from point-in-time observations.
- Create model registry, simple baselines, rolling/holdout evaluation and permanent forecast archive.
- Keep probabilities and predictive claims suppressed until statistically defensible.

**Exit:** reproducible historical comparisons, transparent coverage gaps and no-lookahead checks.

## Phase 3 — Investment research breadth

- Research eleven GICS sectors and constituent industries with source-qualified earnings, valuation, benchmarks and relative total returns.
- Expand to company, ETF, bond and commodity research only as licensing, coverage and evidence permit.
- Add general hypothetical scenarios, not personalized portfolios; include costs, risk and survivorship disclosures.
- Archive theses and evaluate outcomes without hindsight edits.

**Exit:** no fabricated rankings; qualified general investment research and traceable outcomes.

## Phase 4 — Global research and AI collaboration

- Introduce international indicators and cross-border shock mechanisms prioritized by demonstrated U.S. relevance.
- Provide source-grounded AI explanation and research proposal workflows.
- Gate autonomous model changes and publication behind deterministic tests and approval.
- Audit privacy, cost, data licensing, security and model failure modes.

**Exit:** reproducible global research and AI that cannot publish unsupported or personalized recommendations.

## Phase 5 — Product maturity and commercial readiness

- Research accountability center with longitudinal evaluation and transparent corrections.
- Improve user testing, accessibility, reliability, security, performance, monitoring, cost controls and support.
- Obtain securities counsel's review of actual services, subscriptions, claims, exemptions/registration and disclosures before commercial investment-research launch.
- Release only independently verified capabilities.

**Exit:** reliable user-tested platform, complete audit trail and required legal review.

## Prioritization rules

1. Integrity and publication correctness before new predictions.
2. User comprehension and research transparency before cosmetic complexity.
3. Source coverage and licensing before investment ratings.
4. Baselines and holdout evidence before predictive probabilities.
5. General research and compliance by design before monetization.
6. No parallel pipeline or unnecessary rewrite; large coherent chunks, automated tests, explicit done criteria.

## Planning realism

Building the software is feasible in stages; proving superior forecasting skill is uncertain and cannot be promised. Data licensing, point-in-time histories, long-run evaluation, security, operating cost and legal review may determine schedule. No fixed completion date or guaranteed predictive edge is asserted.
