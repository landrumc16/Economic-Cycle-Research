## Observation-month defect (fixed; owner-approved Option 2)
- **Root cause:** `v5_integrated_acceptance.py` required the V5 timeline month (the latest month shared by UNRATE, CPIAUCSL, INDPRO, TOTALSL and BUSLOANS) to equal the monthly gate month (the latest month shared by all eight series, including the slower Case-Shiller and personal income). Publication schedules differ, so fresh data failed the gate (run 44011042484).
- **Correction:** each module is checked against its own sources.
  - The monthly month must equal the assessment's common month and the minimum `latest_available_month` across all sources.
  - The V5 month must equal the timeline artifact's `last_month` and not be later than its own series.
  - Portal and dashboard must agree.
  - Months must be valid; nothing may be later than the assessment month; a used month may not be later than its available month; the assessment must be approved and carry the gate's date.
- **Second finding while verifying:** October 2025 CPI and UNRATE were never published.
  - CPI's 12-month change for 2026-10 was not computable, so the whole judgment was withheld. It now uses the latest computable month (2026-09, at most one month back), dated and noted.
  - On the December run, the V5 timeline will end before its sources. The gate allows that only through the timeline artifact check.
  - The missing prior-year unemployment comparison is shown as an evidence gap.
- **Tests:** 10 new gate tests (the real lag pattern; the December gap; invalid, future-dated, missing and inconsistent dates) and 2 assessment tests (fallback dated correctly; no deeper fallback or imputation).

## Scorecard outcome definitions (clarified 2026-11-17; scores unchanged)
- **Cautious calls** (Weakening, Softening) count as right if unemployment rose ≥ 0.2 pt **or** production growth fell ≥ 1 pt within 6 months. Base rate: 54%.
- **Constructive calls** (Steady, Firming, Strengthening) count as right if unemployment did not rise ≥ 0.2 pt. Base rate: 65%.
- The tests overlap and are not complements. Each share is compared only with its own base rate; lift below 5 pt is labelled "not informative". This is stated on the Historical page, in the scorecard JSON and markdown, and in the review pack.
